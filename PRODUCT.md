# Ember

Ember is a macOS menu bar utility for people who want their display to warm automatically at night. Most of the time it should run quietly in the background. Opening the panel should answer three questions quickly: What light is on now? When does it change? How do I pause it?

The day preview should make the schedule tangible: scrub a time, see it on the display, and return to the current light on release. Keep the app responsive, use native controls, and keep the display in true color until setup and licensing allow tinting. Ember is distributed as a notarized DMG.
