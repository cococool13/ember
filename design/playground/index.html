Questions

Does Ember change color during the day?

No. During daylight at 6,500 K, Ember restores your display profile so your screen shows true color with no tint.

What happens if I use f.lux or Night Shift?

While Ember is on, it quits f.lux and turns Night Shift off. Two tints never stack on your screen.

Can I edit photos with Ember running?

Yes. Photos, Preview, Figma, Photoshop and any apps you pick switch to true color while they are in front.

Is Ember a sleep aid?

No. Ember is a display comfort tool and does not claim better sleep or eye health.

Where do sunset times come from?

Ember uses your location for sunset times. If location is off, it uses a default location.

How the color curve moves

Ember cools to 6,800 K after wake, restores your display profile at 6,500 K during daylight, then drops through 2,700 K toward your night setting. The curve blends smoothly so there are no visible steps.

  1. 7:00 AMWake. Ramp to 6,800 K over 25 minutes.
  2. 8:25 AMSettled at 6,500 K. Gain 1.00 on every channel.
  3. 5:28 PMSunset. 2,700 K by 6:08 PM.
  4. 11:00 PMBed. Night strength holds until wake.

Curve after Brown et al. 2022 and CIE S 026. Times shown for Brunswick, GA on November 21, waking at 7:00 AM.
