# Ember 0.7.0

- New 24-hour dial in the menu-bar panel. Drag it to preview any time of day, or use the arrow keys and VoiceOver.
- Night light now holds the night light until your next wake time. It's also in Shortcuts.
- Automatic updates through Sparkle, with an on/off toggle and Check for Updates in Settings. If you're on 0.6.2 or older, download this release once more.
- Fades now follow your display refresh rate, up from 30 fps to 119 fps on a 120 Hz display.
- The menu-bar panel reopens faster.
