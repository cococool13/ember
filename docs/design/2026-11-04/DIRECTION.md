Mood: Horizon, because the day’s light curve answers the question that brings people to the menu bar: when does the screen change?
Signature: A playhead on the actual warmth curve. Drag to preview, let go to return to now.

[Daybook](concepts/daybook.png) emphasized native light controls and a color strip. [Horizon](concepts/horizon.png) emphasized the schedule. [Ember Dial](concepts/ember-dial.png) emphasized the time of day as a dial, but competed with the interactive track. Horizon keeps one visual focus and follows the system appearance.

The [old panel](before/panel-now.png) placed the phase and preview in one card and strength in another. The [new panel](after/panel-now.png) gives the curve a full-width line and uses system type, a switch, a pull-down menu and a segmented control. The 340 × 365 pt panel keeps the Ember mark and darkens its fill in dark appearance. Muted blue belongs to the curve and its temperature readout; the light appearance uses a darker blue for contrast. Pause, Resume, Activate and Finish Setup occupy the same position. Settings and Quit sit at the bottom.

Apple’s [macOS guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-macos/), [popovers](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/popovers/), and [pull-down buttons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/pull-down-buttons/) informed the narrow control set, standard shortcuts and one related pause menu. The existing Ember panel informed the wake/bed endpoints, preview gesture and branded phase mark.

The curve is drawn from Ember’s actual schedule and night strength. A mouse drag previews the pointed-to time; arrows step a focused graph by 15 minutes; Return and Escape end preview. Motion settings preserve the existing opacity-only presentation. Reduce Transparency replaces the popover material with a solid surface.

Review: the chart and next change remain legible at panel size, including light appearance; the locked panel has no live playhead or enabled preview; the original settings and activation controls are reachable. The chart deliberately omits an axis legend: the temperature reading describes the live point, and the wake and bed labels identify the time range. VoiceOver speech and physical tint were outside the screenshot harness.
