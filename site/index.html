Questions?

How do I buy and install?

Pay through Stripe. Your license key arrives by email. Use Download for Mac, open Ember.dmg, drag Ember to Applications, open it, and paste the key, or unlock with the email you paid with to get a 6-digit code.

Does it work with my display?

External display support depends on the display and connection; HDR and color profiles can affect the result.

What about Night Shift and f.lux?

Ember turns Night Shift off and quits f.lux while enabled.
