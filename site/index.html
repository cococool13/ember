The science, in context

Brighter days.
Dimmer evenings.

Ember adjusts one part of your light environment: the display. Daylight, room lighting, screen brightness, and time before bed still matter.

The Brown et al. 2022 consensus recommends brighter daytime light and much lower light for the three hours before bed. CIE guidance explains why color temperature alone is not a health metric. Ember’s presets do not measure the light reaching your eyes or guarantee better sleep.

A few things to know.

Still have questions? Get in touch.

Which Macs are supported?

The current download is version 0.2.0 for Apple silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later. A notch is not required.

How do I download after paying?

Pay through Whop, then return here and use Download for Mac. Keep your receipt as proof of purchase. Open the DMG and drag Ember to Applications.

Does it work with external displays?

Ember applies color changes through macOS display gamma tables. Support depends on your display and connection. The on-screen result can differ with HDR, a display color profile, or other software controlling display color.

What happens to Night Shift and f.lux?

Ember turns Night Shift off and quits f.lux while enabled to avoid competing color adjustments. Pause or turn Ember off to restore your display’s original color.

Can I pause it for color work?

Yes. Pause gives you one hour of true color, then Ember returns. With True color apps enabled, Photos, Preview, Figma, Photoshop, and other supported apps get true color while frontmost.

Will this improve my sleep or protect my eyes?

Ember does not measure light reaching your eyes or guarantee better sleep. A warm tint is not eye-disease protection. Daylight, room lighting, and your sleep routine still matter. For screen discomfort, take breaks, blink, reduce glare, and adjust brightness for comfortable reading. See American Academy of Ophthalmology guidance.

Does it need an account or collect data?

The app has no account or usage telemetry. Settings and optional location stay on your Mac. This website uses Whop attribution; see the Privacy page.

Is the desktop preview the real app?

The desktop is an interactive illustration, not a connection to your Mac’s display settings. It approximates color and software dimming. The actual panel screenshot below the preview shows the next release; the public download remains version 0.2.0.
