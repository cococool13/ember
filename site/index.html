Questions

How do I buy and install Ember?

Buy Ember for $9 once and download the signed and notarized DMG for macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. Open Ember.dmg and drag Ember to Applications. Activate it with the license key from your email or a 6-digit code sent to the email you paid with.

Does it work with my display?

Ember works on Mac displays and restores your display profile during the day. External displays, HDR, and custom color profiles can affect how the color shift looks.

What about Night Shift and f.lux?

While Ember runs, it quits f.lux and turns Night Shift off. You don't have to turn them off by hand.

How warm and dim does it get at night?

Ember has three night strengths: Gentle at 2200 K and 68% brightness, Standard at about 1800 K and 55%, and Deep at 1600 K and 45%. Standard is the default.

Will Ember help me sleep better?

Ember changes display color. It does not promise better sleep.
