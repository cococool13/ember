Privacy

The menu-bar app does not make an account and does not send usage data. Wake time, bed time, and on/off live in local preferences on your Mac.

To check your license, the app sends your license key and a one-way hash of this Mac's hardware ID to Ember's license server when you activate and about once a week after that. Nothing else is sent.

Location is optional and stays on the device. The app uses it only to compute sunrise and sunset. If you decline, it uses Brunswick, Georgia.

Checkout runs on Stripe's hosted page. Stripe and Link receive your payment details and email; the license server keeps your email and key so it can resend a lost key. The marketing site loads no analytics.
