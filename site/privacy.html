Privacy

The menu-bar app does not make an account. Wake time, bed time, and on/off live in local preferences on your Mac.

The app sends anonymous usage events and crash reports to PostHog (US). They hold no personal data: no name, email, license key, or file names. PostHog discards IP addresses. To stop them, turn off “Share anonymous usage and crash reports” in Settings.

To check your license, the app sends your license key and a one-way hash of this Mac's hardware ID to Ember's license server when you activate and about once a week after that. If you unlock with your email instead of the key, the app also sends your purchase email to that server, which uses it to email you a 6-digit code (through our email provider, Resend) and to check that code. The server stores only a scrambled form of the code, which expires after 10 minutes and works once.

Location is optional and stays on the device. The app uses it only to compute sunrise and sunset. If you decline, it uses Brunswick, Georgia.

Checkout runs on Stripe's hosted page. Stripe and Link receive your payment details and email; the license server keeps your email and key so it can resend a lost key.

This site counts anonymous page views and clicks with PostHog (US), stored in your browser without cookies. It loads nothing when Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control is on.
